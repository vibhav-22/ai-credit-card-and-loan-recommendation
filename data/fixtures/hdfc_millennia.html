HDFC Bank Millennia Credit Card

A flagship cashback credit card designed for online shoppers, dining, and lifestyle expenses.

Cashback & Rewards

  • 5% Cashback on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Swiggy, Zomato, Cult.fit, Tata CLiQ, and Uber.
  • 1% Cashback on all other retail spends and wallet reloads (excluding fuel).
  • Cashback is given as CashPoints, where 1 CashPoint = ₹1.
  • Maximum cashback of ₹1,000 per calendar month on 5% partner category.
  • Complimentary domestic airport lounge access: 1 visit per calendar quarter upon spending ₹1,00,000 or more in the previous calendar quarter.
  • 1% Fuel Surcharge waiver at all fuel stations across India on transactions between ₹400 and ₹5,000.
