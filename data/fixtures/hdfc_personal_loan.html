Who is Eligible?

  • Employees of select private limited companies, public sector undertakings, and government entities.
  • Age criteria: 21 years to 60 years at loan maturity.
  • Net monthly income: Minimum ₹25,000 per month (₹50,000 in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru).
  • Minimum 2 years of overall employment experience with at least 1 year in the current organization.
  • Credit score: 720+ preferred for lower rate tier.
