Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card

The definitive zero-fee cashback card for seamless digital spending across Amazon and merchant partners.

Rewards Structure (Direct Amazon Pay Balance)

  • 5% Cashback on Amazon purchases for Amazon Prime members.
  • 3% Cashback on Amazon purchases for non-Prime members.
  • 2% Cashback on 100+ Amazon Pay partner merchants (recharge, bill payment, Swiggy, Uber, etc.).
  • 1% Cashback on all other retail spending. No cashback on fuel, gold purchases, or EMI transactions.
  • No upper limit or cap on the total cashback earned per month.
  • Cashback never expires and gets credited directly as Amazon Pay balance every billing cycle.

Annual & Joining Fees

Joining Fee: ₹0 (Nil). Annual Renewal Fee: ₹0 (Lifetime Free). No hidden annual maintenance charges.

Finance Charges: 3.50% to 3.80% per month (42% to 45.6% per annum) for revolving credit.

Eligibility Requirements

Applicant must be an Indian resident between 21 and 65 years of age.

Minimum monthly net salary of ₹25,000 for salaried applicants (₹35,000 for non-ICICI account holders). Self-employed with ITR > ₹3.0 Lakhs.
