SBI Xpress Credit Personal Loan

Low-interest personal loans for salaried employees maintaining accounts with State Bank of India.

Financial Features and Charges

Loan Amount Offered₹25,000 to ₹20,00,000 (Maximum up to 24 times Net Monthly Income)
Interest Rates11.15% to 14.30% p.a. (floating, tied to 2-year EBLR)
Tenure Options6 months to 72 months
Processing Fee1.50% of the loan amount plus applicable GST (Minimum ₹1,000, Maximum ₹15,000)
Prepayment Penalty3% on prepaid amount if prepaid from other sources; 0% if closed through another SBI loan

Eligibility Guidelines

  • Permanent employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Educational Institutions, or select Corporate entities.
  • Salary account must be maintained with State Bank of India.
  • Minimum net monthly income: ₹15,000.
  • Applicant age: Minimum 21 years and maximum 58 years.
  • Minimum CIBIL score: 650+.
